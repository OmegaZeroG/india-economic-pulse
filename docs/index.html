Limits

  • The current industrial output series is short, and it cannot be joined to the old one: their growth rates correlate only weakly.
  • Most results rest on a few dozen monthly points, so small differences are within noise.
  • Recent GST and fuel figures are provisional, and a Diwali that moves between October and November distorts year-on-year comparisons.
  • Data is downloaded by hand and loaded with Python and SQL. There is no live feed and no live forecast.
